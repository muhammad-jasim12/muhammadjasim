the Islamabad–Rawalpindi metropolitan area,, [b] commonly referred to as Islamabad–Rawalpindi or the Twin Cities, is the third-largest metropolitan area of Pakistan, after Karachi, and Lahore. With a population of over 5.7 million, it is the second-most populous metropolitan area in the Punjab region, the largest in northern Punjab, and third in the entire country.
